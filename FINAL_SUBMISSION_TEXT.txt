Project: Cookie Alpha Radar

Cookie Alpha Radar is a Cookie Chain-native market intelligence and transaction-preparation cApp. It combines CookieScan market data, explainable Alpha and Risk scoring, Nightly wallet connectivity, Cookie Chain wallet intelligence, and dual-router Cookiebox/Candy Shop quote comparison.

The execution path is non-custodial: the app performs a server-side fresh re-quote, validates route identity/output deterioration and the v0 transaction fee payer, then supports wallet review/signing, Cookie RPC simulation, broadcast and confirmation handling. The submission demo does not claim that the author personally broadcast a mainnet transaction; no chain signature is presented unless one is genuinely produced by a user.
